What drivers, delivery, cleaners & freelancers leave on the table

What Alexis sees every year: drivers, delivery, cleaners, and freelancers pay for the work out of their own pocket — and most of it is deductible. Here is where the money usually hides.

Mileage

Every business mile counts. The finder uses the standard rate — 70 cents a mile for 2025 — and turns your driving into real deductions. Keep a log; it adds up fast.

Phone

Your phone is your office. The part of the bill that’s business is a deduction — the finder counts half of it, every month.

Supplies & gear

Bags, chargers, cleaning supplies, uniforms, tools — the things you buy to do the work. Save the receipts.

Quarterly taxes, in plain words

Alexis puts it plainly: nobody takes taxes out of your payouts. That part is on you — four estimated payments a year.

Set a share aside

Every time money comes in, put a share aside for taxes before you spend it. That one habit is most of the battle.
